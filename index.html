About the company

Reliable crews for vessels worldwide

Kalliston Energy Group Ltd is a maritime recruitment agency specialising in the selection and placement of professional seafarers. We supply shipowners and ship managers with qualified deck officers, engineers and ratings across all vessel types.

Every candidate we present is screened against strict competence, documentation and medical standards. Our mission is simple: the right crew, on time, with full confidence on both sides of the gangway.

  • Rigorous document & certificate verification
  • Experience-matched to vessel type and trade
  • Transparent process for seafarers and owners
